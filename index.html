Calibration

The app checks its own math against your scale.

Every calorie burn figure is an estimate. Fitnouriva plots your real weigh-ins against the weight its ledger predicted. When they drift apart, it adjusts your burn estimate to match your body.

Δweight × 7,700 kcal/kg = intake − scale × burn

Solved for scale over a rolling four-week window, then applied to every screen at once.

The correction can't tell a logging slip from a burn error, and water weight doesn't follow 7,700 kcal/kg. It works best with steady logging.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a smartwatch?

No. Workouts, food and weight all work on their own. A watch or your phone's step counter adds steps, heart rate and measured burn through Health Connect, which makes the daily budget more accurate.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 and newer, installed from Google Play. There is no iPhone version.

Pounds or kilograms?

Either. One switch in Settings changes weight, height, distance and pace together, and your history converts with it.

How does the photo recognition work?

A model on your phone suggests its top five dishes with a confidence score. You pick one, adjust the portion, and the nutrition comes from the food library. The model never invents a calorie figure, and the photo stays on your device.

What if a barcode isn't found?

The app takes you straight to label scanning. Photograph the nutrition label, check the fields it read, and the food is ready to log. You can choose to share it so it's reviewed and added for other people.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

It stays on your phone. You can export it or delete it at any time, even after your subscription ends. If you backed up to Google Drive, you can restore it later.
